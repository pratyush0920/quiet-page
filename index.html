    For the unsaid part

    You do not have to make the feeling easy for anyone else.

    If the only honest sentence is “achha nahi lag raha,” that is a complete sentence. You do not need a neat reason, a positive ending, or a plan before the feeling is allowed to count.

    Being capable does not cancel tiredness. Being strong does not mean every day has to look strong. And being cared for does not create a debt. You are allowed to receive warmth without immediately returning it.

    Why this exists
