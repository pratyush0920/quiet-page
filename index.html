    Meri aankhon se.

    Kuch cheezein jo maine tumhe kabhi boli nahi. Ya boli, par theek se nahi.

    Tum har baar bolti ho, “text me once you reach.” Aur phir sach mein wait karti ho. Tum sabko exit deti ho, “aapke plans hain toh aap jao,” aur phir chupke se umeed rakhti ho ki koi ruk jaye. Tum “kuch nahi chahiye” bolti ho, aur usi hafte kisi ke liye ghanton laga kar sahi shade dhoondhti ho. Tum “itsok” bolti ho jab theek nahi hota, aur agle din khud aakar poochti ho ki doosra kaisa hai.

    Tumne ek baar khud ko clumsy bola tha. Jo insaan doosron ka itna dhyaan rakhta hai, woh clumsy nahi hota. Bas apne liye time nahi nikaalta.

    Jab kuch accha hota hai toh tumhara pehla sawaal hota hai, “pakka?” Kyunki tumhe aadat nahi hai ki cheezein tumhare liye aasaan hon. Par tumne har accha din khud kamaya hai. Aur jab koi tumhare liye kuch karta hai, toh tum “itna mat karo” bolti ho, jaise koi hisaab chal raha ho. Koi hisaab nahi hai. Tum lene layak ho. Bas yeh maan lo.

    Ek raat, jab mujhe sach mein kisi ki zarurat thi, tum do baje jaag rahi thi aur tumne sirf itna poochha, “are you ok?” Tumhe shayad yaad bhi nahi hoga. Mujhe hai.

    Tumse baat karke cheezein halki ho jaati hain, bina tumhare kuch khaas kiye. Yeh tumhari sabse badi cheez hai, aur tumhe iska pata hi nahi tha. Ab hai.

    Yeh sab maine kabhi bola nahi. Ab likh diya. Jab bhi khud pe shaq ho, yahan aa jana. Yeh page nahi badlega.
